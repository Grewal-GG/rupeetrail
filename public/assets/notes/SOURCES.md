# Banknote reference images

Source: [Reserve Bank of India museum](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/pm_republicindia.aspx), accessed 8 October 2026. Images retain RBI's ownership and are bundled as denomination references for this personal journal. They do not represent the exact saved note or identify its series.

The original combined photographs remain unchanged. `note-faces.js` uses separate SVG viewports for the left (front) and right (back) halves, displaying one face at a time. The SVGs embed the source raster images; they are not vector redrawings and do not increase the source resolution.
